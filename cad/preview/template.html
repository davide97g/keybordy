DECODING MESHES…

Print order

  1. 01-fit first. Check that a switch clips into the 14.1 mm hole, that the cap holds on the stem, and which knob bore (1–3 notches) fits the encoder shaft.
  2. Caps and plate next (02, 03, 04). They don't depend on the PCB.
  3. Deck and knobs (05), then the tray (06). The tray's USB and switch cutouts follow the layout, but the PCB isn't designed yet: treat this tray as the prototype.

On the bed

  • Caps, knobs, plate and deck print top face down, so their tops pick up the plate texture and every bore's mouth is the last layer.
  • The tray prints floor down; its 4° slope is in the walls.
  • No supports anywhere. Wash the plate before each print and watch the first layer.
