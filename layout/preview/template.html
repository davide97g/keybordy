ESP32-S3 macropad: 22 MX keys in three sizes, three knobs, a white 2.42″ OLED, a mic and a speaker. Black case, white caps, gray mods, one green talk bar. Every part is printable on the A1 in Bambu PLA Basic.

Shape

The top deck prints face down on the Textured PEI plate, so the top surface gets the plate's fine texture with no sanding. The caps float 3 mm above the deck, showing the switches.

Caps carry only their number, K1 to K22, so any keymap fits without reprinting. The OLED names a key's action on each press, and LAYER (K19) swaps the whole map.
