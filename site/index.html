01

22 keys.

Hot-swap MX switches in three sizes: 1u, 1.5u and a 2u talk bar on a screw-in stabilizer. Each cap carries only its number, so any keymap fits without reprinting a thing.

  • Kailh hot-swap sockets
  • 5×6 matrix
  • USB HID or Bluetooth

02

1 screen.

A white 2.42″ OLED names every key's action the moment you press it. No legends to memorise, no cheat sheet taped to the desk.

  • SSD1309 · 128×64
  • Layer, link, battery
  • Shows each action as you press

03

3 knobs.

Volume, scroll, brightness, whatever you want. Scroll this page and they turn. 24 detents a turn, and every knob also clicks.

VOL42

04

your voice.

Hold the green bar and talk. The mic streams to your Mac, gets transcribed, and the intent runs as an action. A speaker answers back with sounds, speech and notifications.

  • I²S MEMS mic, hardware kill switch
  • 2 W speaker
  • Hold space to try it

05

every layer.

A custom PCB that JLCPCB assembles, inside a case printed in one piece on a Bambu Lab A1. Every part comes from one layout file, so the CAD, the PCB, the firmware and this page all agree.

  1. Knobsprinted, D-bore
  2. Top deckprinted face down, PEI texture
  3. KeycapsPLA, waterslide legends
  4. SwitchesMX, hot-swap
  5. Plate1.5 mm printed
  6. PCBESP32-S3, all SMD on one side
  7. Battery & speaker1000 mAh LiPo
  8. Case133 × 138 mm, 4° typing angle

Make it yours

map every key.

Each key runs an action on your Mac: open an app, run a command in a terminal or a shell, open a URL, fire a Shortcut, send keystrokes, type text or control media. A local editor maps them, and the screen shows what you set.

Build log

Where it's at.

keybordy started as eight switches jumper-wired to an ESP32 DevKit. MP is the real thing. Progress so far:

  1. done
    keybordy v0

    8 keys on a DevKit as a Bluetooth keyboard, F13–F20 driving Mac actions through Hammerspoon, plus a local simulator.

  2. done
    Layout, pins and 3D concept

    One layout file feeds the PCB, the CAD, the firmware pin header and the keymap editor.

  3. done
    Printed parts

    Case, deck, plate, caps and knobs modelled in build123d, fit-checked and sliced for the A1.

  4. done
    PCB rev A

    Placed, routed and exported with Gerbers, BOM and pick-and-place for JLCPCB assembly.

  5. next
    Bench prototype

    Firmware bring-up on breakout boards: matrix, USB and BLE HID, encoders, OLED, audio.

  6. soon
    Voice and keymap v2

    Whisper on the Mac, intents, text-to-speech, and an editor that draws itself from the layout.

  7. then
    First boards on the desk

    Order, print, assemble, and film the real thing.
